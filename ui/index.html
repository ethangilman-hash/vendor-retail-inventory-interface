OREGON MENU RESEARCH · OCTOBER 24, 2026

Inventory category guide

Menu categories, product attributes, and your planning groups serve different purposes.

This is a practical Oregon preset drawn from a small public-menu sample and Dutchie documentation—not a statewide frequency ranking, sales ranking, or verified API mapping. Some menu details were available only in search-indexed pages; live subcategory visibility was incomplete. A Dutchie online menu does not by itself verify which POS the retailer uses.

Eight main inventory categories

Main categorySubcategories / planning groupsMapping notes
FlowerBulk / Loose Flower; Pre-PackagedYour ounce-special and $3/$5/$7/$9/$12/$14-per-gram planning tiers are preserved. Strain type and weight are separate attributes.
Pre-RollsNon-Infused; InfusedSingle and multi-pack are packaging formats. Keep your four planning sections.
VaporizersCartridge; All-in-One / Disposable; PodDistillate, Live Resin, Cured Resin, and Rosin are extract attributes. A live-resin AIO is counted once, in the AIO section.
ConcentratesLive Resin; Cured Resin; Rosin / Live Rosin; Badder / Budder; Shatter; Diamonds / Sauce; Hash / Kief; RSO / FECOA practical mix of product types and planning groups, not an exact list of Dutchie global subcategory IDs. RSO defaults here; retain each store’s original mapping.
EdiblesGummies; Chocolates; Drinks; Baked Goods; Hard Candy / Mints; Capsules / Tablets; Syrups / Drink MixersSingle gummy and multi-gummy packs remain separate planning sections. Syrups may be mapped differently by store.
TincturesDrops / OilsSeparated from Edibles. Specific source subcategories must be confirmed from the connected retailer catalog.
TopicalsLotions / Creams; Balms / Salves; Transdermal PatchesAdded as a separate category. Groupings are a working preset and require store-level review.
AccessoriesBatteries / Hardware; Papers / Cones; Glass / ToolsNon-cannabis inventory stays separate from cannabis categories. Store names and tax/compliance mappings are not overwritten.

Store-dependent categories and attributes

CBD, Apparel, Seeds, and Clones appear on some menus and can be enabled when a retailer carries them. They are not assumed stocked in this pilot. CBD is treated as a cross-category attribute by default to avoid counting the same product twice.

Each integrated product should retain its source category and subcategory, alongside a reviewed mapping to our category, product format, extract type, package size, and planning tier. Unmatched items require review; this update does not modify any retailer’s Dutchie account.

Research sources

Dutchie category configuration · Dutchie subcategory guide · Cannabend · Bend · Salem Organics · Salem · OG Collective · Salem · Substance · Springfield · Lemonnade · Milwaukie · Dark Matter · Eugene

Strong recurring categories: Flower, Pre-Rolls, Vaporizers, Concentrates, Edibles. Tinctures, Topicals, and Accessories recur across several menus; visibility varies by store and inventory. Subcategory frequency could not be measured reliably, so the groups above are recommendations, with store-specific overrides required.
